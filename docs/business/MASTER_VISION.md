# Master Vision

## Vision
Build a trusted event-commerce and operations platform based on real-world event experience, initially for Ice Rinks Australia and expandable to other operators.

## Initial market
- Pop-up ice rinks
- Winter festivals
- Touring attractions
- Session-based family events

## Expansion
Christmas events, sporting events, shows, exhibitions, food and wine festivals, schools and corporate bookings.

## Advantage
Glacier is designed around gate queues, session capacity, family rules, equipment hire, live attendance and multi-city operations.
