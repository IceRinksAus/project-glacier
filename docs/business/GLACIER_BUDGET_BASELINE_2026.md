# Glacier Budget Baseline — Entity Formation to Pilot Operation

## Status and purpose

Draft budget baseline prepared 28 August 2026 for organiser, accountant and Sprint 31 review. All amounts are Australian dollars and generally include GST where a public price includes it. Professional-service and infrastructure figures are planning allowances, not quotations.

This model answers three different questions:

1. What cash should be reserved to establish Glacier correctly and reach a responsible pilot?
2. What will Glacier cost each year before staff, marketing and transaction fees?
3. Which costs grow with ticket and payment volume?

The model must be refreshed after entity advice, dated provider estimates, insurance quotations, legal scope and pilot-volume assumptions are available.

## Executive budget position

The original independent-entity estimate combined establishment, professional legal work, production hardening and independent security assurance. It was a prudent end-to-end reserve, not the minimum amount needed immediately.

Revised structural scenarios are:

| Pathway | One-off through controlled pilot | Incremental annual fixed cost | Main trade-off |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Independent Glacier entity now | $35,000–$60,000 | $25,000–$40,000 | Clean separation at highest immediate cost |
| Permanent Ice Rinks Australia division/product | $12,000–$30,000 | $10,000–$25,000 | Lower cost, but Glacier assets and liabilities remain inside the existing operator |
| Documented incubation then spin-out | **$15,000–$35,000 before spin-out** | **$12,000–$27,000 during incubation** | Lower immediate cost while preserving a planned separation path |
| Lean internal pilot with assurance deferred | $6,000–$15,000 | $7,000–$18,000 | Suitable only for a tightly controlled internal pilot; not evidence of commercial production readiness |

The recommended planning path for professional evaluation is documented incubation within Ice Rinks Australia, with separate Glacier accounting/asset records and a defined spin-out gate. Allow an additional **$8,000–$20,000** around the eventual spin-out for entity establishment, advice, assignments, account transitions and contingencies; the amount depends heavily on how well the incubation records are maintained.

Across every pathway, payment processing remains volume-based—approximately 2.0% at a $100 domestic-card average order under the current standard rate.

The reserves are not predictions that every dollar will be spent. They prevent legal, privacy, security and recovery work from being abandoned because only company-registration and hosting fees were budgeted.

These totals exclude founder salary, employees, customer support labour, advertising, event operations, ticket proceeds payable to organisers, tax, financing costs and the historical value of software already created.

### 1.4 Contingency

Reserve 15–20% against the selected professional and technical scope. Legal changes, trademark objections, security remediation, additional payment-provider work and pilot-device issues should not consume operating cash unexpectedly.

### Recommended funding gates — independent entity now

Use the following staged approvals rather than spending the upper allowance immediately:

| Gate | Release when | Suggested reserve |
|---|---|---:|
| A — Entity and ownership | Accountant/lawyer scopes approved | $8,000–$15,000 |
| B — Brand and legal operating terms | Entity/name direction confirmed | $7,000–$15,000 |
| C — Staging and security hardening | Correct cloud account holder confirmed | $8,000–$15,000 |
| D — Independent assurance and rehearsal | Staging is stable and feature scope frozen | $15,000–$30,000 |

Several gates overlap. A **$35,000–$60,000 total cash reserve** is a prudent current planning range for immediate independent establishment through a controlled pilot, with expenditure authorised gate by gate.

### Costs avoided or deferred during documented incubation

If professional advice confirms Glacier can be incubated inside Ice Rinks Australia, the following may be avoided or deferred:

- new proprietary company registration and annual review;
- separate annual accounts, tax return, bookkeeping platform and registered-office administration;
- separate banking and some merchant-account establishment;
- some duplicate insurance, email/identity and corporate administration;
- immediate transfer of existing software and development assets; and
- part of the entity/related-party legal work until commercial viability is clearer.

The following are not removed by incubation:

- Glacier-specific domains, brand and asset records;
- cloud hosting, backups, monitoring and production operations;
- secure payment and privacy controls;
- customer terms, refund/Flexible Ticket wording and Waiver review;
- confirmation that Ice Rinks Australia's insurance covers the software/pilot activities;
- independent security assurance appropriate to the live risk; and
- eventual transfer/assignment work if Glacier is spun out.

During incubation, Ice Rinks Australia should maintain a Glacier cost centre, asset register, contributor/IP evidence, supplier/account list and separately identifiable revenue/expenses. This is an accounting/legal design question, not a conclusion of this budget.

## 3. Variable and transaction-linked costs

### Stripe online payments

Stripe's current Australian standard pricing displays:

- domestic online cards: 1.7% + $0.30 per successful transaction;
- international cards: 3.5% + $0.30;
- an additional 2% where currency conversion is required; and
- no standard monthly/setup fee.

The original card-processing fee is generally not returned when a card payment is refunded. Contracted/custom pricing may differ.

At the domestic rate, illustrative processing cost is:

| Average order | Fee per order | Effective rate |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $25 | $0.73 | 2.90% |
| $50 | $1.15 | 2.30% |
| $100 | $2.00 | 2.00% |
| $200 | $3.70 | 1.85% |

Illustrative domestic-card cost at a $100 average order:

| Annual card sales | Orders | Approximate Stripe cost |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $100,000 | 1,000 | $2,000 |
| $500,000 | 5,000 | $10,000 |
| $1,000,000 | 10,000 | $20,000 |
| $5,000,000 | 50,000 | $100,000 |

This must be modelled separately from Glacier revenue. Whether the organiser, customer or Glacier economically bears processing costs is a commercial and legal pricing decision.

### In-person payments and hardware

Stripe currently displays domestic Terminal processing at 1.7% + $0.10, with listed readers from approximately $89 to $469 before GST and optional cellular service at $15 per reader/month. Linkly/bank and Square alternatives require current quotes including terminal rental, merchant service fees, integration certification/support and replacement-device arrangements.

### Other variable costs

- transactional email and SMS per message;
- cloud compute, storage, logs, backups and outbound traffic as usage grows;
- chargebacks/disputes and fraud losses;
- refunds where original processing fees are retained;
- customer support labour per booking/event;
- accounting and settlement work if Glacier handles organiser funds;
- hardware per venue/gate/till; and
- future Stripe Connect/platform fees if Glacier becomes a marketplace/payment platform.

## 4. Costs deliberately excluded from the current total

- the market value or replacement cost of software already built;
- founder time or salary;
- future employees and contractors;
- sales, marketing, travel and customer acquisition;
- event-specific operating costs borne by Ice Rinks Australia or another organiser;
- tax liabilities and working-capital requirements;
- customer refunds and organiser settlement balances;
- external fundraising/investor legal work;
- international trademark/company expansion; and
- major post-pilot feature development.

These exclusions must enter the future Financial Model before Glacier's pricing, break-even point or funding requirement is approved.

## 5. Immediate actions to convert estimates into a decision budget

1. Send the ownership brief to the accountant and request a fixed or capped quote plus establishment options.
2. Obtain a commercial-law quote split into entity/IP, customer terms/privacy, Waiver and payment-platform questions.
3. Choose the likely trade mark classes with professional advice and save the official fee calculation.
4. Approve or revise the proposed Google Cloud architecture, then create dated low/expected/peak calculator estimates.
5. Request two independent security/penetration-test scopes after staging exists.
6. Obtain insurance indications after the contracting entity, revenue path and data profile are known.
7. Record actual Codex/ChatGPT and other development-tool expenditure from account invoices.
8. Create a volume model using expected events, tickets, average order value, domestic/international mix, refund rate and POS share.
9. Review this baseline quarterly and before any live-pilot commitment.
